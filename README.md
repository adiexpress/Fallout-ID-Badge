I decided to choose this design because I really like cars and especially the Lamborghini Veneno, the car that this PCB was inspired by.
<img width="525" height="380" alt="venenogit" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/41181887-08c6-4e33-a890-dad0089687b3" />

I wanted to keep it relatively simple, but cool enough that I could flex and show off and people would be like "oh shoot, thats sick!", so I used LED's to act as headlights and illuminate the car

Since I used LED's, I also needed a battery source (the CR2032) and 2 resistors (both 470V)

To create the border, I traced out a side-by-side picture of a sketch I found online
<img width="8000" height="5624" alt="Ad Car" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/645e232b-2dd0-4810-8371-6b6f1f53a8eb" />

And for the details, I tried my best to replicate the sharp edges and curves of the car and I think I did a pretty decent job

I really liked making this small project and I will definetly keep this PCB as memoria for Fallout 2026
